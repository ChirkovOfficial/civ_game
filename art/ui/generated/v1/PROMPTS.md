## Основной запрос

Use case: ui-mockup. Asset type: production-oriented raster UI sprite atlas for a historical 4X strategy game in Godot. Generate ONE large high resolution square texture atlas, ideally 4096x4096, with genuine transparent background between all components. This is a sheet of isolated reusable UI assets, NOT a screenshot or assembled game screen. Style: refined historical strategy interface, deep midnight navy enamel, restrained antique brass edging, small turquoise accents, ivory details, subtle parchment texture only in tooltip panels. Clean orthographic front view, sharp consistent thin borders, attractive at small sizes, minimal ornament and generous dark empty interiors for future dynamic Russian text. No text, letters, numbers, logos, maps, scenery, faces or baked-in labels anywhere.
Orderly rows with generous transparent gutters, no touching or overlapping objects. Include a COMPLETE coherent kit:
TOP QUARTER: one long empty top resource HUD strip; six separate compact resource counter capsules color coded cyan science, purple culture, gold currency, green food, orange production, pale faith; two research/civic cards with empty circular icon wells, empty progress tracks and blank text areas; one wide empty city name banner with circular population socket; one slim notification banner.
SECOND QUARTER: a large empty city-management panel with title bar, tabs and empty content body; a tall production chooser panel with title bar, three tabs and blank content body; a wide low city overview panel with large circular crest socket and empty metric rows; a rectangular minimap FRAME with a completely TRANSPARENT central opening, small corner flourishes and separate zoom controls; one empty unit command panel and one parchment tooltip panel.
THIRD QUARTER: twelve separate district production row plates, each a dark navy horizontal bar with a small colored hexagonal medallion at left and small empty circular turn socket at right; medallions depict campus flask blue, holy lotus pale silver, encampment shield red, harbor anchor teal, commercial coin gold, theater lyre violet, industry gear orange, entertainment pavilion amber, housing house green, aqueduct arches green, baths water cyan, government column slate. Empty row centers. Three rectangular button state triplets normal / hover / pressed plus muted disabled button, four tab shapes, four empty circular portrait frames, four empty hexagonal badge frames, several empty narrow city map-label banners in red blue green and gold.
BOTTOM QUARTER: separate empty progress tracks AND separate fill strips in science cyan, culture violet, production orange, food green, health red; separate circular progress ring segments; round end-turn button with arrow and normal hover pressed variants; small individual utility buttons close X, checkmark, back arrow, next arrow, plus, minus, gear, question mark, magnifier, pin, crossed swords, shield, hammer, scroll, globe, diplomacy handshake, save disk, folder, hourglass. Small isolated yield icons science flask, culture lyre, production gear, food wheat, gold coin, faith star. Horizontal and vertical scrollbar tracks and thumbs, checkboxes on/off, radio buttons on/off and thin panel dividers.
Every item must be individually cuttable. Keep rectangular panel edge centers simple and straight for future nine-slice stretching. Hollow frames truly transparent inside. Filled panels have opaque navy interiors. Preserve even spacing and crisp silhouettes, no background checkerboard baked into image. Exclude nuclear weapons, combat aviation and climate/emissions imagery.

## Уточнение

Edit the provided UI atlas. Keep the same arrangement, all panels, icons, buttons, dimensions and navy/brass design. Clean up ONLY transparency and edges: remove ALL stray colored red/green/cyan/yellow fringes, glows, speckling, smears and detached pixels surrounding the assets and between rows. All empty gutters must be genuinely fully transparent alpha zero, not black or a checkerboard. Every asset must have a crisp clean silhouette with restrained antialiasing at its actual edge. Minimap interior remains transparent. Keep panel interiors navy and tooltip parchment. Do not add text. No glow, no drop shadows outside silhouettes, no particles. Produce the complete cleaned atlas with ample transparent margins.
